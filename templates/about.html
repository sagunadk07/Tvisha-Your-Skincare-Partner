About Tvisha

Skincare that starts with understanding your skin.

Tvisha pairs a trained AI model with dermatology-informed recommendations, so you get an honest read on your skin and a care plan that actually fits it — no guesswork, no generic routines.

Our mission

Most people choose skincare products by trial and error. We built Tvisha so anyone can get a clear, photo-based read on what their skin actually needs — then match it to ingredients and products that are backed by evidence, not marketing.
